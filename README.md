#### Start here

- **[stanford-dining-menus](https://matmat202.github.io/stanford-dining-menus/)**: Every Stanford dining hall menu for the week, on one page, built for phones. Unofficial.  
  *Refreshes itself every day on GitHub Actions; keeps a history of which dishes each hall serves.* `scraper` `pwa` `leaflet` · [code](https://github.com/matmat202/stanford-dining-menus)
- **[where-did-i-park](https://matmat202.github.io/where-did-i-park/)**: Works out where you parked from your speed and position, and points you back. Installable, offline, nothing leaves the device.  
  *Tells a red light from a parked car by whether you then walked away: no map data, accelerometer or Bluetooth, and no server.* `pwa` `geolocation` `offline-first` · [code](https://github.com/matmat202/where-did-i-park)
- **[room-now](https://matmat202.github.io/room-now/)**: What class is in every Stanford room right now  
  *All 346 rooms on the course schedule, worked out from the public catalogue.* [code](https://github.com/matmat202/room-now)
- **[daily-ngrams](https://matmat202.github.io/daily-ngrams/)**: How often The Stanford Daily printed a word, 1892–2014: a Google Ngram for one campus paper  
  *About 695,000 articles from 1892 to 2014, one hit count per word per year.* `ngrams` `data-visualization` · [code](https://github.com/matmat202/daily-ngrams)
- **[name-years](https://matmat202.github.io/name-years/)**: Every US first name, every year since 1880: a chart from Social Security records  
  *Its tests check the charts against the raw Social Security files (Matthew, 1990: 44,826 births).* `data-visualization` · [code](https://github.com/matmat202/name-years)
